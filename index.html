Getting started

  1. Choose a service. Copy its MCP endpoint into your client as a remote Streamable HTTP server.
  2. Explore free information. Use get_payment_info without buying credits. Services 003 and 002 also provide get_service_info.
  3. Purchase credits when needed. Follow the service's /buy flow. The buyer's wallet sends native USDC on Base, pays gas, and signs a redemption challenge. You receive a bearer token.
  4. Make protected tool calls. Add your bearer token securely to the MCP Authorization header. A successful completed paid operation spends the stated credits.

For compatibility details and tool inputs, inspect each service's OpenAPI document.

Built to fail safely.

Clear limitations matter when money is involved.

Independent, experimental tools. These services are not affiliated with Base, Coinbase, or Circle. They are not custodial wallets, payment processors, financial institutions, or accounting software. They only inspect on-chain data and use prepaid access credits; they do not initiate transfers for you. Payment proof requires agreement from two public RPC providers and safe finality. If the providers are unavailable or disagree, a query can fail instead of returning an assured answer. That is a safety property, not automatic high-availability failover. This is not x402 and no seller-side wallet key is used. The credit purchase flow asks your own wallet to sign a redemption message, never to disclose a seed phrase or private key.

This site is informational, has no analytics, cookies or third-party scripts, and does not collect wallet credentials. Public health, OpenAPI, purchase-page and free MCP information checks passed on October 7, 2026. Paid operations were not tested. Check live payment information before purchasing.
